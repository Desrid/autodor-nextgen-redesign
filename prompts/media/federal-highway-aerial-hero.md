# Federal highway aerial hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: full-width homepage hero and road-network slide source
Primary request: a cinematic but credible aerial view of a modern federal motorway crossing a vast northern Eurasian landscape
Scene/backdrop: multilane divided highway flowing through mixed forest and open terrain, a distant river bend and low horizon, clear late-summer atmosphere
Subject: engineering scale of the road network, precise interchanges, barriers, drainage and realistic sparse traffic
Style/medium: premium documentary aerial photography, physically believable optics and materials, restrained editorial color grade
Composition/framing: wide horizontal establishing shot; the motorway creates a strong continuous diagonal-to-depth route; keep the central 55 percent coherent for a 4:5 mobile crop while retaining calm peripheral landscape for desktop UI; no essential subject at the extreme edges
Lighting/mood: bright low morning sun, clean air, subtle atmospheric depth, confident and calm rather than dramatic or ominous
Color palette: natural greens, mineral gray asphalt, pale blue sky, restrained warm sunlight; compatible with a light white, cool-gray and orange interface
Materials/textures: plausible asphalt aggregate, clean lane paint, galvanized barriers, concrete structures, varied vegetation
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no readable road signs, no license plates, no invented official symbols; lane geometry and markings must be physically plausible; no distorted vehicles; enough resolution and visual detail for a wide website hero and responsive crops
Avoid: generic stock-photo polish, oversaturated HDR, fantasy roads, impossible junctions, empty decorative coordinates, HUD graphics, cyberpunk color, orange-purple grade, heavy fog
