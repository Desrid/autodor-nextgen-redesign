# Tunnel portal

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: homepage gallery and road-network slide source
Primary request: a modern dual-carriageway road entering a carefully engineered tunnel portal in a rocky forested cutting
Scene/backdrop: stable rock slopes with realistic reinforcement, mixed forest above, drainage channels and a bright daylight exit atmosphere around the portal
Subject: safe, precise tunnel infrastructure with two clearly separated bores, realistic lanes, barriers and restrained traffic
Style/medium: premium civil-infrastructure documentary photography, photorealistic and naturally detailed
Composition/framing: wide horizontal ground-level-to-elevated perspective facing the portal at a slight angle; preserve both tunnel bores and the approach road inside the central 55 percent for a 4:5 mobile crop; leave calm rock and vegetation margins for desktop UI
Lighting/mood: crisp bright daytime exterior with controlled dark interiors, no ominous mood; natural exposure that preserves detail in concrete and rock
Color palette: mineral gray, asphalt black, natural forest green, pale sky and warm safety-light accents only where physically plausible
Materials/textures: shotcrete, reinforced concrete, rough rock, clean asphalt, galvanized barriers, realistic lane paint
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no readable road signs, no license plates, no invented official symbols; physically plausible portal geometry, road markings, clearance and drainage; no distorted vehicles
Avoid: horror atmosphere, cinematic smoke, neon light, cyberpunk tunnel, fantasy geometry, glowing route overlays, excessive HDR, decorative signage
