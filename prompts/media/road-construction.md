# Road construction

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: homepage gallery and construction story source
Primary request: an active large-scale motorway construction site showing coordinated roadbed, paving and interchange work
Scene/backdrop: broad infrastructure corridor through open terrain, partially completed carriageways, drainage and an overpass structure in progress
Subject: real civil-engineering activity with paver, rollers, excavator and a small number of properly equipped workers at useful working scale
Style/medium: premium documentary construction photography, photorealistic, candid and technically credible rather than staged advertising
Composition/framing: wide horizontal elevated viewpoint with construction flow leading into depth; keep the main paving operation and workers within the central 55 percent for a useful 4:5 mobile crop; retain layered site context across the desktop width
Lighting/mood: bright overcast or soft morning daylight for legible materials and safe working visibility; purposeful, measured and optimistic
Color palette: earth and aggregate neutrals, asphalt gray, concrete, restrained safety orange and yellow, natural landscape colors
Materials/textures: graded aggregate, fresh asphalt, compacted soil, realistic machinery wear, concrete and steel
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no readable road signs, no license plates, no invented official symbols; machinery proportions and worker PPE must be credible; safe separation between people and operating equipment; plausible road geometry and construction sequence
Avoid: staged handshakes, heroic propaganda pose, unsafe workers, duplicated machinery, impossible paving workflow, oversaturated HDR, cyberpunk, HUD overlays, fake technical labels
